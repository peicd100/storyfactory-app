Privacy Policy — StoryFactory Uploader

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Who uses this app

This app is used only by the owner of a single YouTube channel, on the owner's own computer, to manage that same channel. No other users are served.

What data it accesses

Through YouTube API Services, the app may upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel and read the channel's own statistics and YouTube Analytics reports (for example views, watch time, traffic sources and countries).

How data is stored and used

  • Data is used only to help the owner understand and manage the channel.
  • Reports are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared, or sent to third parties.
  • Google authorization tokens are kept only on the owner's computer in encrypted form.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

Third-party terms

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/peicd100/storyfactory-app/issues.
